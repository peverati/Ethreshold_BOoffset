## Files

* `make_figures.py`: one function per figure (`fig1` … `fig8`) with one shared style.
  * Size: PCCP single column 8.3 cm or double column 17.1 cm.
  * Text: Arial, 7 pt (6–6.5 pt ticks, 5–5.5 pt annotations).
  * Output: vector PDF (TrueType fonts embedded) and 300 dpi PNG.
  * Colours: Okabe–Ito palette (colour-blind safe).
  * Symbols: exact = filled circles; BO = dashed; BO+ad = dotted.
* `make_tables.py`: T1–T5 as `tables.md` and `tables.tex`. The LaTeX is booktabs `table*`; it compiles without overfull boxes at a 17.1 cm text width (tested 2026-11-17, all five tables).
* `summary_values.py`: the numbers quoted from `paper_data/RESULTS_SUMMARY.md` (critical masses, markers, muonic data, asymptotic constants, Kutzelnigg points). `check_against_data()` compares them with the raw files: **562 checks, all agree** (2026-11-17; 106 before runs 5 and 6, plus 434 for `threshold_map/` and `unequal_masses/`: every hard-coded number of `RUN5`, `J7V0`, `RUN6`, `R1_CHECK` against RESULT.md (per-level sections, summary table, complete map, blind-comparison table), `data/*_crossings.csv` (best and certified bounds), `bo_map.json`, `prediction_map.json`, `prediction_unequal.json`, the sha256 of PREDICTION_MAP.md, PREDICTION_UNEQUAL.md and prediction_unequal.json, the L = 6 one-sided-error rule, PASS flags, 14/14 and 0/7 counts, and the 9 calibration levels of `prediction_map.json` against T1; plus 22 for (7,0): the J7v0 note of RESULT.md, `threshold_map_L7hp/data/J7v0_crossings.csv` (best bound, certified bound at tol 1e-14, excluded N = 8 + 2 diffuse row, monotone compact sequence), its place in the order of appearance and the 14/14 count). Earlier:  (38 earlier + 68 for `paper_data/thresholds_Lv/`: values parsed from RESULT.md, minima of `*_crossings.csv`, the M* implied by `*_binding.csv` and its E_b/(M−M*) table, `prediction_J1v1.json`, the sha256 of the frozen PREDICTION_J1v1.md, offsets = BO+ad − M_c, and the muonic thresholds = T1 values). `make_tables.py` runs it before writing.

Status labels used below:
* **rigorous**: variational bound or exact limit;
* **extrapolated**: threshold-law or basis extrapolation;
* **fitted**: least-squares fit;
* **derived**: parameter-free prediction from our computed functions;
* **converged numerical**: basis-converged variational energies and observables;
* **literature**: value as published; **converted**: literature parameter converted by us.

## Design choices (and why)

* **F1** uses the binding energy on a log scale, not $E-E_{th}$ on a linear scale. Binding energies span 1e-11 to 0.1 Eh, so only a log axis shows the levels being born at $M_c$. Levels $v\ge2$ exist in our data only within about 1 mass unit above $M_c$, from the critical-mass runs: no mass scan of $v\ge2$ was ever made. Their curves are therefore short, nearly vertical segments. BO levels are not drawn: only BO $v=0$ and $v=1$ are tabulated in `mass_scans/bo.csv`, and new BO runs would be new calculations.
* **F2(a)** is drawn log-log, so the slope-1 law $\sqrt{E_b}\propto x$ is visible over three decades. The fit lines were fitted here ($M_c$ fixed). They are not the production fits of `fits_state*.txt`, but they agree with them ($c_1$ within 0.3%).
* **F5(a)** also shows $E-E_{BO}$ with its $\kappa^4$ law, for contrast. Both series are exact-minus-model differences: circles for BO+ad, squares for BO to tell them apart. Lines follow the convention (dashed = BO-related law, dotted = BO+ad-related law). **F5(b)** has an inset because exact, BO and BO+ad are indistinguishable on the main axes for M ≥ 50.
* **F4(b)** has one row per marker: the 0.2–1.4 window is too crowded for a single strip. The fidelity marker (1.39) and the entanglement band (0.357–0.475) are included as "this work". RESULTS_SUMMARY §7 quotes the ground-state span as 0.2–1.11 (0.32 widths); with 1.39 it is 0.36 widths (T2 footnote gives both).
* **F7** is a matrix (level existence only), as requested. No binding energies are plotted.
* **F3 (2026-11-17)**: the central figure is now the offset test (`F3_offsets`). Panel (a) of the old `F3_massfunc_offsets` ($m_v(R)$, $m_r(R)$) did not fit next to three offset panels at 17.1 cm, so it moved unchanged to its own single-column figure **F8_massfunc**; the old `F3_massfunc_offsets.{pdf,png}` were moved to the Trash (no document references the filename except this README). The offsets are plotted against the exact $M_c$ (not a category axis) so that 23 levels fit and the slow drift of the offset with $v$ and $J$ is visible; the residual panel (b) carries the pass/fail information. Panel (c) uses $\|$exact − model$\|$ on a log axis because theory misses (0.04–0.18) and null misses (0.5–11) differ by one to two decades; the sign (all theory residuals negative) is stated in the panel and in T5.
* **F1(b)** is a new bound-state map on a linear $M$ axis (0–103) rather than more lines in panel (a): 14 more labelled thresholds do not fit on the log binding-energy panel. The run-5 binding curves in (a) are drawn grey and unlabelled.
* **F4 / T2** keep the nine earlier thresholds only: they describe the atom-to-molecule crossover region; the run-5 thresholds (27.7–97.4) are in F1(b) and T4.
* **L = 6 values (superseded; see `../threshold_map_L7hp/J6_note.md`)**: value = best proven upper bound (`best proven upper bound (up to rounding)` of RESULT.md: 66.983757757, 97.416774043) with a one-sided error σ− = max(|best − extrapolated| + extrapolation error, largest step of the quoted compact-size sequence) = 2.6e-4 and 4.6e-4. This rule is ours; `summary_values.py` recomputes it. The differences and PASS use this value (−0.0012, −0.0025; RESULT.md, using the extrapolation, gives −0.0013, −0.0025).
