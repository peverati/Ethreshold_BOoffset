## 1. Exact critical masses for excited bound states of {X+,X+,e-} (symmetric S states, J = 0)
Source: `paper1/critical_masses/` (README, fits_state*.txt, upper_bounds.csv, fig_state*.png). Code: `solvers/threebody/threebody/threshold.py`.

**PRIORITY (added 2026-11-16; supersedes every "no literature value known" statement below).** The threshold masses of the lowest excited levels were first located by Frolov and co-workers in 1989 to 1994 (finite exponential bases of 600 to 1500 functions, 3 to 5 digits, no error bars): Frolov-Yeremin, J. Phys. B 22, 1263 (1989) (read; 2 1S, 3P, 2 3P thresholds stated without method); Bishop-Frolov, J. Phys. B 25, 3049 (1992) (read; P and D brackets from 700/800-function calculations); Frolov 1992 II, J. Phys. B 25, 3059 (read, tables checked); Frolov-Smith-Bishop, PRA 49, 1686 (1994) (read); Frolov-Wardlaw, EPJD 63, 339 (2011) (read). Frolov 1992 II also gives the order of appearance (1S, 3P, 2 1S, 1D, 2 3P, ...; so "rotation before vibration" for the antisymmetric level is his observation), reduces the near-threshold problem to an ion bound to a polarizable atom (credit for our threshold-law/halo picture), and Frolov-Bishop 1992 conjecture a zero-slope binding law at the boundary; the ion-plus-atom reduction is already in Bishop-Frolov 1992 (Eq. 9), which also states a quadratic threshold law for P and D (wrong; ours is linear for L > 0). The value 11.485 is a rounded estimate in a footnote of BF92 Table 2 (not in FY89; the attribution to FY89 in Frolov-Bishop PRA 1992 is wrong).

## 2. Two separate "molecule-like" scales
- Literature: ground-state descriptors change character near M ≈ 0.4–1 (Mátyus et al. 2011 density about the CM: transition interval 0.4 < m_a/m_b < 0.8, full text read 2026-11-13; King et al. 2013 binding-ratio minimum at m1/m3 = 0.491; Salas et al. 2022 sign change near mass ratio ≈ 1, our interpolation ≈ 0.98 (0.984 to 0.987 depending on the interpolation; rests on one small positive second-derivative value at R = 1 of a single-term four-parameter trial function without error analysis; the authors state only "R about 1"; read in full 2026-11-16, no pre-emption of our claims)).
- Our first excited-state threshold: M ≈ 9.53. So the ground-state distribution changes shape about an order of magnitude in mass before the system can support a vibrational excitation.

## 5. Solvable models (`solvers/harmonic_model/`, 49 tests)
- Both models (all-harmonic "A"; harmonic + g/R^2 "B") separate exactly into nuclear × electronic parts: electron–nuclear (R|ρ) entanglement is identically zero, so they are only a null test for that measure. A non-separable benchmark is still needed.
- Fidelity: a plateau in the globally dilation-invariant fidelity susceptibility is NOT a signature of structure (model A, structureless, has a plateau 3/64 > model B's 1/32). The "separate-dilation" susceptibility (independent rescaling of nuclear and electronic coordinates) is 0 for structureless model A and for two bodies, 1/32 for model B; 1/32 equals the BO localization exponent, i.e. generic, not critical.
- Density topology: about the pair midpoint, model B has a hole at every M; about the centre of mass, a hole appears only above M_c (0.633 for g = k = 1), and M_c moves from 4.69 to 0.032 as g goes from 0.01 to 100. Reference-point dependence shown in closed form.
- Lindemann ratio ΔR/<R>: constant 0.422 in model A (no structure), ∝ M^(-1/4) in model B. Good descriptor.
- Rigid-rotor estimate of the rotational spacing from <R^-2>: 23% off at M = 1, 0.03% off at M = 1836 (model B, exact tower available).

## 6. Rotational (L = 1, 2) levels, exact (`solvers/threebody`, `paper1/rotational/`)
Formulation: bipolar-harmonic expansion (Bhatia & Temkin 1964; Drake 1978), natural parity, exact integrals, mass-independent matrices. 32 tests pass (rerun independently).
- Validation against references verified online: He 2^1P, 2^3P to 1e-10 (Drake & Bondy, arXiv:2606.00814); H2+ (v,L) = (0,0),(1,0),(0,1),(1,1),(0,2),(1,2) to 1e-12 to 1e-13 (arXiv:1407.1973, m_p = 1836.15267247). H2+ (0,0) = -0.597139063079 at that mass; the remembered -0.597139063123 is correct for the older proton mass 1836.152701 (we get -0.5971390631232 there; paper1/king2013), not an error.
- Symmetry (derived): the ground-manifold rotational tower has X-exchange symmetry (-1)^L (L=0,2 symmetric, L=1 antisymmetric). Threshold for all natural-parity L: E_th = -M/(2(M+1)).
- Rotational critical masses (lowest state of each L binds): L=1 (antisym.) proven upper bound 4.2082830, extrapolated 4.2082827 ± 5e-7; L=2 (sym.) upper bound 11.4894218, extrapolated 11.4894217 ± 3e-7. BO: 5.5374983, 12.7397228; BO+adiabatic: 4.5705653, 11.7786164. Near these thresholds binding grows linearly (centrifugal barrier), no halo.
- Earlier estimates of the L=1 and L=2 thresholds (4.219, ~4.20, 4.2133; 11.485 (BF92 estimate inside its bracket 11.4 to 11.5), bracket 11.489 to 11.490; (1,1): 17.128) are in the PRIORITY block of section 1; ours are converged to 7 to 8 digits. The ordering L=1 < v=1 < L=2 < (1,1) was already given by Frolov 1992 II.
- Rotation appears BEFORE vibration (already observed by Frolov 1992 II, with finite bases): the first rotational excitation (L=1) is bound for M > 4.21, the first vibrational excitation (v=1, L=0) only for M > 9.53; the full tower {L=0,1,2} exists only for M > 11.49. Run 4 adds L=3 (21.376199(1), antisym.) and L=4 (33.914349(2), sym.) as lowest levels of each L (linear threshold laws, slopes 1.712e-3 and 1.211e-3 Eh per mass unit), the (J=1, v=1) level at 17.094282(1) (between L=2 and L=3; threshold law linear with visible curvature) and, exploratory, the lowest L=1 symmetric (2p sigma_u-like) level at 813.54 ± 0.07 (see §1, run 4 block). The tower of lowest levels {L=0,...,4} thus exists only for M > 33.91.
- Spacings: d1 = E(L=1)-E(L=0); M*d1 rises from 0.154 (M=5) to 0.487 (M=1836) (rigid rotor 2<1/R^2>: BO 0.4878 at M=1836). Ratio d2/d1: 2.475 (M=20), 2.749 (50), 2.864 (100), 2.932 (206.8), 2.992 (1836), approaching the rigid-rotor 3 from below; BO reproduces the ratio almost exactly (centrifugal distortion). BO+adiabatic d1 relative error ~ +0.07/M for M >= 20 (spot-checked, not fitted).
- Done in run 4 (§1): L=3, L=4 lowest levels, (J=1, v=1), and (exploratory) the lowest L=1 symmetric level (threshold 813.54 ± 0.07). Not done: antisymmetric L=0 (2p sigma_u J=0; BO critical mass 330.62, exact not computed), L=1 symmetric excited roots, unnatural parity; relabelling invariance test.
